# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, stay on topic, and give constructive feedback. Do not harass, insult, threaten, or
publish another person's private information. Avoid sexualized content, discriminatory language,
and personal or political attacks.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. It also applies
when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior violates this policy.

Use GitHub's **Report content** action to report abuse or harassment. Do not post private evidence in
an issue. GitHub documents confidential abuse reports at
[Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
